# The report

End with a short report for the reader, in their language:

- **Done**: what you changed, as a list of outcomes rather than a list of files, with the important files named.
- **Verified**: the commands you ran and their results, including the test and coverage numbers.
- **Not done**: anything you deliberately left out or could not do, and why, and anything unrelated you noticed that deserves attention.
- **Next**: only when there is a real decision or action for the reader.

Keep it brief and factual. Do not repeat the plan, do not paste large diffs, and do not claim anything you did not verify.
